Ballot Check

Look up your official ballot, copy the research brief this page builds for you, and paste it into an AI assistant with web search on (tested with ChatGPT and Claude; some assistants, like Gemini, decline election questions). You get a plain summary of everyone on your November 3, 2026 ballot: their record, who funds them, and whether they keep their promises.

1. Where do you vote?

Your address stays in this page. It is only added to the text you copy below. A ZIP code works, but a full address finds your exact local races.

2. Start with the official ballot

Look up the real list of races and measures first. These are free and nonpartisan.

Vote411 ballot Ballotpedia lookup Vote.gov

AI assistants cannot look up your exact districts from an address. Copy the list of races (and candidates, if shown) from the official ballot lookup above and paste it here. This makes the results far more accurate.

3. Get your research brief

Pick a length, copy the text, and paste it into an AI assistant that has web search on (tested with Claude and ChatGPT). If an assistant declines, use the official links above instead.

Check the AI's numbers yourself

  • FEC.gov and OpenSecrets: federal campaign money
  • FollowTheMoney: state and local donors
  • PolitiFact and FactCheck.org: claims and promises
  • Vote Smart: voting records and candidate answers

AI can make mistakes. Confirm dates, deadlines, and your polling place with your county elections office.
